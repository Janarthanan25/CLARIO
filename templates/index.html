The Problem & The Solution

Eliminate Fragmented Tools with One Cohesive AI OS

Modern knowledge workers waste hours switching between document analyzers, inbox clients, and disconnected fraud detection dashboards. CLARIO unifies all three mission-critical domains into a synchronized, multi-agent AI ecosystem.

A. CLARIO AI Workspace

Drag-and-drop document intelligence supporting PDF, DOCX, TXT, and CSV. Context-grounded Q&A strictly citing uploaded file content, automated summaries, key date tracking, and instant CSV charting.

  • Multi-format text extraction
  • Grounded contextual queries
  • Automated Chart.js CSV analytics

B. Smart Email Assistant

Instant email processing with categorical sorting (Important, Finance, Urgent), sentiment classification, task and deadline extraction, and multi-tone reply drafting.

  • Professional, Friendly & Formal tones
  • Task & deadline extraction
  • Human-in-the-loop review control

C. CLARIO Security AI

Financial transaction surveillance combining deterministic rules (duplicate charges, velocity bursts) with Scikit-Learn IsolationForest machine learning anomaly detection.

  • Hybrid ML & rule-based scoring
  • Clear LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH risk ratings
  • Explainable fraud alert reasoning
